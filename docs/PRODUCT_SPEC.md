# TeamWeave — Product Specification

**Codename:** Living Weave  
**Category:** Intelligent Team Formation and Collaboration Platform  
**Target Audience:** Hackathon Organizers, Participants, Mentors, Judges

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Hackathons frequently suffer from ineffective team formation:
- **Random / Blind Grouping:** Creates unbalanced teams (e.g., four backend engineers with no frontend or design capability).
- **Popularity & Clique Bias:** Experienced attendees cluster together, leaving beginners and solo participants isolated.
- **Inflated Metric Credentialism:** Platforms over-index on GitHub commit counts, excluding designers, non-code contributors, or junior engineers.
- **Team Attrition Frustration:** When a member drops out midway, teams struggle to find replacements without administrative friction.

---

## 2. Product Solution
TeamWeave delivers a comprehensive, explainable solution:
1. **Low-Friction Onboarding:** 4-tier skill model (Exploring, Developing, Independent, Advanced) coupled with voluntary evidence linking.
2. **Deterministic Matchmaking:** Requirement-driven team generation maximizing skill coverage across teams.
3. **Signature Team Blueprint:** A visual, transparent contract showing role distribution, requirement coverage matrix, and explainability notes.
4. **Resilient Reshuffling Studio:** 1-click member withdrawal and instant replacement recommendation based on lost capability impact.
5. **Honest Gamification:** Daily challenges, continuous streak tracking, and milestone badges that reward consistency without false credential inflation.
6. **Post-Event Feedback Loop:** Qualitative reviews on collaboration, distribution of effort, and communication.

---

## 3. User Roles and Capabilities

### Participant
- Browse and join hackathons.
- Declare skills across 5 domains (Frontend, Backend, AI/ML, Design, DevOps).
- Store evidence artifacts (GitHub, Figma, Live URLs, Papers).
- Solve daily challenges and track engagement streaks.
- View assigned Team Blueprint and teammate contribution breakdowns.
- Submit post-event collaboration feedback.

### Organizer
- Create and manage hackathon events.
- Define hard requirements and preferred skill targets with proficiency floors.
- Review registered participant pool and capability distribution.
- Execute Operation B matching engine to generate balanced teams.
- Inspect and fine-tune Team Blueprints.
- Handle withdrawals and approve ranked replacement candidates.
- Publish teams to participants with one click.
